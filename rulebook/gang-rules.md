# ETERNITY SERVER — Gang Rules
*Where Every Story Becomes Eternal.*

> **Draft for server-owner approval.** These rules and penalties are proposed server policy. Confirm all thresholds and timings with management before publishing.

## 1. Gang Registration and Leadership

1. Every gang must be formally registered and approved by the Hood Organizer before operating as an official gang.
2. Gang leadership is responsible for member conduct, scenario coordination, compliance with event limits, and the accuracy of registration details.
3. Follow any registration, roster, meeting, and activity requirements announced by management. Do not represent an unapproved group as an officially registered gang.
4. Management may review or revoke a gang's status for serious or repeated violations, subject to the server's review process.

## 2. Gang Bases

1. A gang base is allocated only after approval by the Hood Organizer and subject to availability. The allocation decision is final unless management reopens it for review.
2. Do not begin or conclude a roleplay scenario at the gang base. Do not use the base to evade police, reset a scenario, or obtain an unfair advantage.
3. Takeovers must not be conducted on a gang's own base. Robbery scenarios must not be moved to, or intentionally concluded at, a gang base.
4. Keep base activity discreet and consistent with roleplay. Do not deliberately expose the base to law enforcement as a way to provoke or manipulate a scenario.
5. During an active scenario, do not access vaults or base storage to replenish equipment or otherwise gain an advantage.
6. Base fees are due every 15 days. If payment is missed, management should issue a final reminder; continued non-payment may result in the base being reclaimed.
7. Base access may be suspended or revoked following confirmed rule violations or Gang Life Point penalties.

## 3. Gang Life Points (GLP)

Each registered gang has a maximum of **250 Gang Life Points (GLP)**.

### 3.1 Deductions and review
- Confirmed FRP and other approved gang-level violations may result in GLP deductions. The deduction should be based on the severity, evidence, prior warnings/bans, and the Gang Organizer's review.
- An allegation alone is not proof and should not result in a deduction without review.
- A published rule may specify a fixed deduction. Management should document the decision and the evidence supporting it.

### 3.2 GLP thresholds
- **Below 200 GLP:** remove applicable tier perks.
- **Below 150 GLP:** restrict special vehicles and suspend gang roleplay activities for two weeks.
- **Below 25 GLP:** the gang must dismantle, subject to management confirmation and review.

### 3.3 Rejoining after dismantling
1. Members of a dismantled gang may join another gang after a **14-day cooldown**.
2. Members may not carry over the former gang's identity or use it to bypass the dismantling decision.
3. A new gang registration may be submitted after **30 days**, using a clearly distinct identity and subject to approval.

### 3.4 Earning GLP
GLP may be awarded for participation in approved underworld events, including illegal car expos, boxing events, and illegal races. Management sets the awarded points based on attendance, participation, and event performance; points are not automatic unless announced.

## 4. Third-Party Involvement

1. Do not initiate, join, or interfere in an ongoing gang scenario unless your gang is officially involved or management has approved your involvement.
2. Do not provide reinforcements, lookouts, weapons, vehicles, tactical support, or other assistance to influence another gang's active scenario.
3. Do not interfere in an active or preparing gang situation, takeover, or gang discussion. Only the gangs involved should communicate and handle the conflict unless management approves otherwise.
4. Example: if Gang X and Gang Y are in a conflict, Gang Z must not join or support either side without approval.
5. The supplied draft lists a **three-day gang suspension** for third-party interference and, in a later rule, a **100 GLP deduction**. These penalties overlap; management must confirm whether they apply together or are alternatives before publication.

## 5. Gang Roster and Event Limits

### 5.1 Street fights
- Minimum participants: **15**.
- Maximum participants: **25**.
- When the event has 25 members, the stated vehicle limit is **8 vehicles total**.
- Only **4 PIT vehicles** may be from premium packs.

### 5.2 Main war
- Maximum format: **12 vs. 12**.
- Vehicle allowance: **4 premium PIT vehicles and 1 common vehicle**, according to the supplied draft.
- Organizers should confirm vehicle counting, eligibility, and permitted equipment before each war.

### 5.3 Civilian-versus-gang scenarios
- These scenarios are prohibited unless approved as a roleplay scenario by management.
- The supplied draft caps civilian participants at **4**.
- The original wording does not clearly state the maximum number of gang participants; management must specify that limit before this section goes live.
- Unauthorized civilian-versus-gang situations carry a proposed **15-day ban for all involved**.

## 6. Gang Fight Scheduling

1. **Monday to Friday:** gang-versus-gang fights are prohibited during city events. A gang must not initiate or engage in such a fight without prior management permission.
2. **Saturday and Sunday:** organized gang fights may take place only after registration and approval.
3. The war-zone location should be selected jointly at the official meeting. The supplied draft lists **Sandy Shores or Paleto Bay** as allowed locations.
4. Do not use an unapproved location or change the agreed location during the event to gain an advantage.

## 7. Takeovers and Gang Conquest Activity (GCA)

### 7.1 Starting a takeover
1. GCA locations may be conquered only through an approved takeover. A gang cannot claim a location for free or simply announce ownership.
2. Two teams must formally initiate the takeover under the approved process.
3. A gang may attempt to take a second GCA location only if it already controls one GCA location.
4. If a location is abandoned, it returns to the Hood Organizer for reassignment.

### 7.2 Zone rules
1. The designated war zone must be clearly communicated before the event begins.
2. Do not shoot from outside the zone. If a player briefly leaves the zone to check position, the supplied rule allows **10 seconds to return**.
3. Shooting, fighting with vehicles, or PIT manoeuvres from outside the zone are prohibited.
4. The supplied draft says players inside the zone may shoot players outside it and that outside-zone campers may be force-killed. Because this conflicts with the general prohibition on outside-zone engagement, management should define exactly how this exception works before publishing it.

### 7.3 Duration and result
1. The listed GCA duration is **45 minutes**. Participants should follow the start and end call given by event staff.
2. The supplied draft states that if the defending team has **50% or more alive when time ends**, the defenders win.
3. Attackers must eliminate **more than 50% of the defenders** to conquer the location.
4. The supplied wording says the fight may continue after the time ends, but does not clearly define when that applies. Management must specify whether the 45-minute limit ends the fight or permits a staff-authorized finish.

### 7.4 Head blast / rejoining
If a player is head-blasted during GCA, the supplied rule says that player may not rejoin. If they rejoin, the GCA is awarded to the opposing team. Confirm the intended meaning of “head blast” and enforcement process before publication.

## 8. War and Breach Cooldowns

- After a main war or breach with the **same gang**, the stated cooldown is **3 days**.
- After a war or breach, wait **6 hours** before starting a war or breach with a **different gang**.
- Management should clarify when the cooldown starts and whether cancellations or interrupted scenarios count.

## 9. Gang Conduct and Shared Assets

1. Gang RDM is prohibited. Proposed penalty: **4-day ban**.
2. Using another gang's vehicles or assets without permission is prohibited. Proposed penalty: **3-day ban**.
3. A gang's own assets may be used inside the city when **two or more gang members are online**, as stated in the supplied draft.
4. The source wording says FRP in these asset scenarios should not be considered when fewer than two members are online. This is ambiguous and should be clarified by management rather than treated as a blanket exemption from FRP rules.
5. Do not exploit vehicles, base access, storage, zone boundaries, or roster arrangements to gain an unfair advantage.

## 10. Evidence and Staff Decisions

1. Staff may request POV/evidence when reviewing a gang scenario. Players must provide the requested recording within the period specified by staff.
2. Players should record before PvP scenarios. If a player cannot record, the supplied draft says they should remain with nearby teammates who are recording; management should confirm whether this is an acceptable alternative to an individual POV.
3. Low-spec hardware is not automatically a reason to disregard an evidence request. Explain the issue to staff and follow the review process.
4. Staff may remove a player from the scene immediately when necessary, depending on the severity of the alleged violation.
5. Administrative decisions are made by the Admin Team. Players may submit a ticket for further review; do not disrupt an active scene to argue a ruling.

## 11. Proposed Penalty Reference

| Violation | Proposed consequence from supplied rules |
|---|---|
| Gang RDM | 4-day ban |
| Unauthorized use of another gang's vehicles/assets | 3-day ban |
| Unauthorized civilian-versus-gang scenario | 15-day ban for all involved |
| Third-party interference | 3-day gang suspension and/or 100 GLP deduction — management must resolve overlap |
| GLP below 150 | Special-vehicle restrictions and two-week gang RP suspension |
| GLP below 25 | Gang dismantling |
| Unauthorized GCA rejoin after head blast | GCA awarded to the opposing team |

Penalties are drafts and should be checked against the server-wide punishment table. Where two rules overlap, management should publish one clear outcome.

## 12. Final Notes

All gang rules, participant limits, GLP deductions, and event procedures are subject to owner approval. Management should resolve the flagged ambiguities before this page is made official.
