# ETERNITY SERVER — Convoy Rules
*Where Every Story Becomes Eternal.*

> **Draft for server-owner approval.** This page separates convoy procedures from the general and gang rulebooks. Confirm the final event process and penalties before publishing.

## 1. Event Authorization and Roleplay

1. A convoy event operates under an active **Section 144 authorization**, as specified in the supplied server rules. Management should confirm the in-server meaning, activation procedure, and who may authorize it.
2. Participants must maintain believable roleplay throughout the event. Unrealistic driving, trolling, desync abuse, and forceful attempts to bypass the scenario may be treated as rule violations.
3. Do not interfere as an outside party unless you or your group has been officially included in the scenario.
4. Combat logging or deliberately disconnecting to avoid the consequences of the convoy scenario is prohibited.
5. The event must be handled through the agreed RP process. Staff may disqualify participants or apply the server's relevant punishment rules for confirmed violations.

## 2. Police Department (PD) Rules

During an authorized convoy scenario:

1. PD may engage in a shootout at any location during the convoy when the scenario and Section 144 authorization permit it.
2. PD may approach from any direction to intercept or stop the convoy.
3. PIT manoeuvres are permitted against convoy/family vehicles during the event.
4. PD may disable convoy vehicles and then proceed with tactical engagement, following the active scenario and the server's roleplay standards.
5. Air support and coordinated interception may be used where available and consistent with the event's authorization.
6. Police participants must still maintain the server's general RP standards and must not use the event as a justification for unrelated or arbitrary action.

## 3. Gang / Family Rules

1. Follow the route assigned by Black Squad or the designated event organizer.
2. Do not change the route without authorization or deliberately avoid the approved route to evade the event.
3. Maintain realistic driving and roleplay. Do not use exploitative manoeuvres, unreachable locations, or map/environment abuse to make the convoy impossible to engage.
4. Families may defend the consignment using their permitted manpower and strategy, while remaining within all announced participant, vehicle, and equipment limits.
5. Do not hide convoy vehicles in unreachable or exploitative locations.
6. Do not bring an outside gang or family into the scenario as an unapproved ally.

## 4. Rules for All Participants

1. **No revival during an active gunfight.** Follow the server's downed/death rules until the active combat situation has ended and the scenario permits recovery.
2. Do not abuse safe zones or use them to escape an active convoy engagement.
3. Third-party alliances are prohibited unless approved before the scenario begins.
4. Do not troll, abuse desync, or deliberately disrupt the event. Keep actions consistent with the agreed RP setting.
5. Any participant who violates the event rules may be disqualified and may receive the applicable server punishment.
6. Staff decisions during the live event must be followed. Submit evidence and use the ticket/review process afterward rather than interrupting the scenario.

## 5. Scenario Boundaries and Enforcement

- The official route and event conditions should be announced before the convoy begins.
- Only authorized participants should engage with, support, or obstruct the convoy.
- Event staff should clarify the start and end of the active gunfight and when revival or other post-scenario actions are permitted.
- The supplied source does not define a fixed standalone ban length for every convoy violation. Apply the general server punishment table or an expressly announced event-specific penalty; do not invent a new duration at the scene.

## 6. Proposed Penalty Reference

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Outside-party interference / unauthorized alliance | Disqualification and applicable server punishment; confirm any fixed duration with management |
| Combat logging | Apply the server-wide combat-logging rule |
| Revival during active gunfight | Disqualification and applicable server punishment |
| Safe-zone abuse or exploitative hiding/driving | Disqualification and applicable server punishment |
| Repeated or severe disruption | Staff review; consequence based on the published server punishment policy |

## 7. Before Publishing

Management should verify the meaning and activation of Section 144, define the authorized event organizer, publish participant and vehicle limits for each convoy, and identify the penalty table used for event violations.
