**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 2 — POV AND EVIDENCE RULES**
*Post 1 of 2*

POV recordings help staff understand what occurred during a disputed scenario. Players should prepare recordings before PvP, preserve evidence, and cooperate with requests.

## 2.1 Mandatory POV Recording
- Ensure recording is active before entering PvP or another high-risk situation.
- If an admin or staff member requests POV during or after PvP, providing it is mandatory.
- Retain footage until the relevant report is resolved.
- Do not fabricate, misleadingly edit, or intentionally withhold relevant footage.
- Follow the submission channel and deadline staff provides.

## 2.2 Low-Spec Recording Requirements
“Low spec” is not, by itself, a valid excuse for refusing a POV request. Prepare a recording setup suitable for your device before joining PvP. The source says that if you cannot record, you should stay with nearby teammates who are recording. Teammate footage is only useful as an alternative if it actually captures the relevant conduct; it is not automatically a substitute for every personal POV.

If a genuine technical failure prevents recording, explain it promptly and provide any alternative evidence requested. Missing footage does not automatically prove or disprove a violation.
