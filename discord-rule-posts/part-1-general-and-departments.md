# ETERNITY SERVER — Discord Rule Posts (Part 1)

*Where Every Story Becomes Eternal.*

Copy each labeled message below into Discord as a separate message. Every message is intended to stay below Discord's 2,000-character limit. Post them in sequence within your rules channels. Markdown tables and headings are retained where practical.

## Copy-ready Discord post 1

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 1 — GENERAL ROLEPLAY RULES**
**Discord message 1/5**

These standards apply to all players and scenarios unless an approved event rule expressly changes a requirement. Keep roleplay believable, continuous, and fair; do not treat every interaction as a contest to win.

## 1.1 General Conduct and Respect
Respect players and staff. Do not derail a live scenario to argue about a ruling; preserve evidence and use the ticket process afterwards. Do not abuse game mechanics, technical problems, or rule loopholes to gain an unfair advantage. **Penalty: 3-day ban; severe cases may be permanent.** Chat-specific rules are not included in this handbook.

## 1.2 Roleplay Standards
Act consistently with your character and the scene. Respond to meaningful events, allow other players a chance to react, and avoid actions that skip required interaction or force a preferred outcome. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.3 Fail Roleplay (FRP)
FRP is conduct that breaks the reasonable logic or continuity of a scene, ignores relevant circumstances, or uses game limitations instead of believable roleplay. Speaking to or interacting with a dead body is treated as FRP in the supplied rules, but the gang rules give a different penalty for dead-body interaction; see Sections 7 and 11. **General penalty: 24-hour ban.**
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 2

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 1 — GENERAL ROLEPLAY RULES**
**Discord message 2/5**

## 1.4 Metagaming (MG)
Do not use information your character did not obtain through roleplay. Information from external chats, streams, recordings, or out-of-character conversations must not influence character decisions until it is established in character. **Penalty: 5-day ban.**

## 1.5 Powergaming (PG)
Do not force an outcome on another player without a reasonable opportunity to respond, claim unrealistic abilities, or use mechanics to obtain an implausible result. Describe the attempted action and allow the roleplay to develop. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.6 Random Deathmatch (RDM)
Do not attack or kill a player without the required roleplay context or valid scenario justification. A rival identity or previous dispute does not automatically justify every later attack. **General penalty: 7-day ban.** Gang RDM is listed as 4 days separately; management must clarify priority when both apply.

## 1.7 Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)
Do not deliberately use a vehicle to hit, run over, or kill players without valid roleplay justification. A permitted PIT manoeuvre in an authorized convoy is a specific event permission, not a general exemption. **Penalty: 3-day ban.**
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 3

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 1 — GENERAL ROLEPLAY RULES**
**Discord message 3/5**

## 1.8 Car Deathmatch (CDM)
Prohibited vehicle attacks intended to harm players or disrupt a scene may be treated as CDM. Staff should consider intent, context, and whether an event expressly permits the manoeuvre. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.** The supplied rule says there is no appeal for clear evidence; evidence must still be reviewed through the administrative process.

## 1.9 New Life Rule (NLR)
After death or when a new-life restriction applies, do not return to the same active situation to continue the conflict or use knowledge your character should no longer possess. Follow staff instructions about when you may return. The source provides no universal radius or timer. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.10 Fear RP / Value of Life
React reasonably to credible danger and value your character's life. Do not ignore serious threats merely to win or escape. Assess the actual threat and available options. Fear for police also applies where the circumstances require it. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.11 Combat Logging
Do not deliberately disconnect or leave the server during an active scene to avoid consequences. If a genuine technical failure occurs, return promptly and explain it to staff; the claim must be assessed against the circumstances. **Penalty: 7-day ban.**
```

```text
## 1.12 Safe Zones
Do not begin prohibited violence in a designated safe zone or use the boundary to escape an active hostile scenario. Follow the zones and restrictions officially announced by the server; this handbook does not add locations. **Penalty: 3-day ban.**

Maintain continuity in character identity and behaviour. Do not switch characters or identities to evade an active scene, consequences, cooldowns, or a gang dismantling. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

Use voice communication in a way that supports the scene and allows participants to understand relevant interaction. Explain genuine technical issues to staff rather than using them for advantage. **Penalty: 12-hour ban.**

Cheats, unauthorized modifications, exploit abuse, and intentional bug manipulation for advantage are prohibited. Stop using an exploit and report it. **Penalty: permanent ban.**

## 1.16 Unrealistic Roleplay (URP)
URP includes plainly unrealistic actions that undermine the setting or exploit game limitations instead of responding through roleplay. **Penalty: 2-hour ban; severe cases may be permanent.**

## 1.17 Cop Baiting
Do not deliberately provoke police to manufacture an artificial pursuit without a reasonable roleplay basis. **Penalty: 3-day ban, no warning.**
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 6

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 2 — POV AND EVIDENCE RULES**
**Discord message 1/2**

POV recordings help staff understand what occurred during a disputed scenario. Players should prepare recordings before PvP, preserve evidence, and cooperate with requests.

## 2.1 Mandatory POV Recording
- Ensure recording is active before entering PvP or another high-risk situation.
- If an admin or staff member requests POV during or after PvP, providing it is mandatory.
- Retain footage until the relevant report is resolved.
- Do not fabricate, misleadingly edit, or intentionally withhold relevant footage.
- Follow the submission channel and deadline staff provides.

## 2.2 Low-Spec Recording Requirements
“Low spec” is not, by itself, a valid excuse for refusing a POV request. Prepare a recording setup suitable for your device before joining PvP. The source says that if you cannot record, you should stay with nearby teammates who are recording. Teammate footage is only useful as an alternative if it actually captures the relevant conduct; it is not automatically a substitute for every personal POV.

If a genuine technical failure prevents recording, explain it promptly and provide any alternative evidence requested. Missing footage does not automatically prove or disprove a violation.
```

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 2 — POV AND EVIDENCE RULES**
**Discord message 2/2**

## 2.3 Evidence Submission
Supply the original or reasonably complete recording with enough context to understand the sequence. Include the scenario, time, and involved players where known. If a file is too large or upload fails, ask staff for an approved alternative. Avoid publishing unrelated private information or interfering with the review. No universal evidence deadline is specified in the source; staff should set one per request.

## 2.4 Administrative Review
Staff should consider recordings, logs, witness accounts, and the rules that applied. An allegation alone is not proof. If evidence is incomplete or accounts conflict, identify what remains uncertain and request more information where appropriate.

## 2.5 Failure to Provide POV
Failure to provide requested POV may result in action under the server policy. The source establishes the requirement but gives no single fixed ban length for every failure-to-provide case. Management should apply the published penalty policy consistently.

## 2.6 Checklist
Keep recording active before PvP, retain original footage, answer requests through the correct channel, explain real technical limitations honestly, and do not fabricate or edit evidence misleadingly.
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 8

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 3 — ROBBERY AND HEIST RULES**
**Discord message 1/3**

Robberies and heists must develop through roleplay, not become immediate unstructured shootouts. Criminals, hostages, and police must respect the same active scenario.

## 3.1 General Requirements
Follow the police-presence and hostage requirements for the robbery category. The supplied rules require PD and a hostage but do not give one universal count. Allow negotiation where required; do not shoot instantly just to skip interaction. Do not involve unrelated players. General robbery violations carry a stated **3-day ban**, subject to the more specific rules below.

## 3.2 Participation From the Start
A player or group must be involved from the beginning of the robbery/heist to participate. Do not arrive late and join because the event looks advantageous. Outside parties may not interfere unless event rules or staff explicitly authorize them.

## 3.3 Police Backup
Backup may be called if all officers involved are down or dead, after a realistic delay for the scene to develop and robbers to collect or proceed. The source gives no fixed universal timer. Do not exploit the delay by camping indefinitely; police may engage through backup where event conditions permit.
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 9

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 3 — ROBBERY AND HEIST RULES**
**Discord message 2/3**

## 3.4 Vehicle Restrictions
Do not swap vehicles through a garage between related scenarios to reset pursuit or escape restrictions. Use permitted vehicles and respect announced limits. Do not use unreachable locations, vehicle exploits, or unrealistic manoeuvres to make pursuit impossible.

## 3.5 Suppressed Fire and Communication
The supplied rules say criminals are not always required to use verbal communication, but suppressed fire is required before firing at PD once pursuit starts. Suppressed fire is also stated to be mandatory if a shootout occurs at the robbery location. Staff should clarify any weapon exceptions before an event. A verbal warning in a civilian-to-civilian confrontation may be considered, but does not itself justify violence.

## 3.6 Cooldowns and Multiple Robberies
The same team must not conduct multiple robberies simultaneously. Observe the relevant category cooldown. The source does not give one universal duration; use the current published rule or event announcement. Do not use an interrupted scenario to bypass a cooldown unless staff confirms it does not apply.

## 3.7 Holding a Robbery Location
The source uses a first-come, first-served approach. If the first team is holding the location while waiting for a cooldown or next permitted stage, a second team must leave. Do not manufacture a shootout over location ownership.
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 11

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 4 — POLICE DEPARTMENT RULES**
**Discord message 1/2**

Police enforce the city's rules while maintaining fair, believable roleplay. Rank, equipment, or police access does not exempt an officer from server-wide rules.

## 4.1 Professional Conduct
Follow department procedures and authorized command instructions. Treat civilians, suspects, hostages, EMS, and other departments professionally. Do not use police status, access, or information for personal disputes or private gain. Every action must relate to a valid RP reason and current scenario.

## 4.2 Appropriate Use of Force
Use only the force supported by the circumstances and applicable SOP. Do not escalate to lethal force without a valid reason; consider whether negotiation, communication, containment, or another reasonable response is available. The source does not include a complete force ladder, so follow current PD SOP and approved event instructions.

## 4.3 Fear for Police
Officers are still roleplay participants and must react believably to credible danger. Do not ignore serious threats simply because you have a badge or equipment. Fear RP applies in accordance with the situation and general rules.

## 4.4 Protection of Police Bodies
The supplied rules specifically prohibit burning police bodies. Do not burn or handle a police body in a prohibited way. If the rule is unclear in a particular scene, avoid exploiting it and follow staff instructions.
```

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 5 — EMS RULES**
**Discord message 1/2**

EMS personnel provide medical roleplay and support emergency response. Their role is medical and neutral; EMS must not act as a combatant or use medical access to influence gang or criminal conflicts.

## 5.1 Medical Responsibilities
Prioritize patient care and follow current department protocols. Maintain believable medical roleplay and do not exploit medical mechanics to force a conflict outcome. The source does not provide a complete treatment SOP or exact stabilization times, so follow the current EMS guidelines for those details.

## 5.2 Neutrality
Do not provide tactical support, gear, or other help to one side of a conflict. Respond according to the medical role and active scene. Neutrality does not mean disregarding personal safety or staff instructions; seek direction if an area is unsafe or a rule unclear.

## 5.3 Government Employee Protection
EMS is protected under Section 10. The supplied rules state that EMS cannot be taken hostage under any circumstances and prohibit unnecessary targeting, random killing, and harassment. Violation of government employee protection carries a stated **15-day ban without exception**.

## 5.4 Revival During Combat
Do not revive a player during an active gunfight where the applicable rule prohibits it. Confirm that combat has ended and the recovery process permits revival. Do not use medical actions to erase consequences or return someone to an ongoing engagement unfairly.
```

## Copy-ready Discord post 15

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 6 — COMMUNITY RULES**
**Discord message 1/2**

Communities and civilian groups must remain peaceful unless management has approved a specific roleplay scenario. Do not use a community as a cover for gang warfare, criminal operations, or coordinated interference.

## 6.1 Peaceful Conduct
Community activity should support ordinary social roleplay. Keep it separate from registered gang warfare and hostile scenarios unless an event is approved.

## 6.2 No Community Gang Warfare
Do not conduct gang warfare under a community name to bypass registration, participant caps, or cooldowns. Changing the label of an active gang conflict does not change the rules that apply.

## 6.3 No Provocation
Do not deliberately provoke or threaten another community to manufacture a hostile situation. A past disagreement does not justify targeting people who are not involved in the current scene.

## 6.4 No Interference in Gang Conflicts
Do not provide reinforcements, lookouts, weapons, vehicles, tactical information, or other help intended to influence an ongoing gang scenario. Do not join because a friend or community member is involved unless you are an authorized participant.

## 6.5 No Forced Recruitment
Do not force or coerce players into joining a community, group, or gang. Recruitment must be voluntary and consistent with RP standards.
```
