**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 1 — GENERAL ROLEPLAY RULES**
*Post 2 of 5*

## 1.5 Powergaming (PG)
Do not force an outcome on another player without a reasonable opportunity to respond, claim unrealistic abilities, or use mechanics to obtain an implausible result. Describe the attempted action and allow the roleplay to develop. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.6 Random Deathmatch (RDM)
Do not attack or kill a player without the required roleplay context or valid scenario justification. A rival identity or previous dispute does not automatically justify every later attack. **General penalty: 7-day ban.** Gang RDM is listed as 4 days separately; management must clarify priority when both apply.

## 1.7 Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)
Do not deliberately use a vehicle to hit, run over, or kill players without valid roleplay justification. A permitted PIT manoeuvre in an authorized convoy is a specific event permission, not a general exemption. **Penalty: 3-day ban.**

## 1.8 Car Deathmatch (CDM)
Prohibited vehicle attacks intended to harm players or disrupt a scene may be treated as CDM. Staff should consider intent, context, and whether an event expressly permits the manoeuvre. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.** The supplied rule says there is no appeal for clear evidence; evidence must still be reviewed through the administrative process.
