## Copy-ready Discord post 1

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 7 — GANG RULES**
**Discord message 1/5**

These rules apply to registered gangs and members acting on their behalf. Leadership must communicate limits and prevent members from bypassing the approved rules.

## 7.1 Registration and Leadership
Every gang requires approval from the Hood Organizer. Leaders must provide accurate registration information and follow roster requirements. They should brief members on event caps, cooldowns, base restrictions, and evidence requirements. Each member remains responsible for their own conduct.

## 7.2 Gang Operational Bases
Bases are allocated by the Hood Organizer based on availability; the allocation decision is final unless reopened by management. Do not begin or end a scenario at your base; takeovers cannot be conducted there; robberies must not be moved there or deliberately ended there. Keep base activity discreet and do not expose it to police to manipulate a scenario. Do not access a vault during an active scenario to replenish equipment. Confirmed violations may lead to GLP reduction or loss of base access.

Base payment is due every **15 days**. If payment is missed and remains unpaid after a final reminder, management may reclaim the base.

## 7.3 Gang Life Points (GLP)
Maximum: **250 GLP** per gang. Confirmed FRP or other approved gang-level violations may cause a deduction after evidence review by the Gang Organizer. Allegations alone are not proof. Document the rule and deduction.
```

**Discord message 1/3**

GCA is the approved process for conquering designated locations. Follow the agreed registration, participant, vehicle, zone, timer, and evidence requirements. Do not claim a location outside this process.

## 8.1 Approved Weapons
The source structure names “Approved Weapons” but does not provide the list. Management must publish the allowed weapons and equipment before each takeover. An item being available in-game does not automatically make it legal for GCA.

## 8.2 Participant and Vehicle Limits
The supplied formats are **main war: 12 vs. 12, 4 premium PIT vehicles and 1 common vehicle**; and **street fight: 15–25 participants, 8 vehicles total with 25 members, maximum 4 premium PIT vehicles**. The source does not make clear whether street-fight counts are per side or combined, or whether both formats apply to every GCA. Announce the active format before participants assemble.

## 8.3 Starting and Claiming
A location may be conquered only through an approved takeover initiated by two teams. A gang may attempt a second GCA location only if it already controls one. An abandoned location returns to the Hood Organizer. Do not claim or occupy a location as conquered without approval.

## 8.4 War Zone Boundaries
The designated zone must be announced before the event. The source says shooting from outside the zone is prohibited; a player briefly leaving to check position has **10 seconds to return**; and shooting, car fighting, and PIT manoeuvres from outside the zone are prohibited. The source also says players inside may shoot players outside and mentions force-killing outside-zone campers. That exception conflicts with the prohibition on outside-zone engagement. Management must resolve the wording and publish one rule before this handbook is final.

## 8.5 Duration
The stated GCA duration is **45 minutes**. Staff should announce the start time and timer-end procedure. The source also says fighting may continue after time ends but does not define whether that is overtime or a staff-authorized finish. Management must decide in advance.

## 8.6 Defending-Team Victory
The source says defenders win if **50% or more of their team is alive when time ends**. Attackers must eliminate **more than 50% of the defenders** to conquer the location. “Last alive wins” also appears in the source. These statements need one hierarchy so the outcome is predictable; management must publish a consistent rule before the event.

```text
**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 9 — CONVOY RULES**
**Discord message 1/3**

A convoy is a structured roleplay event involving an assigned route, a consignment, authorized participants, police interception, and conditions announced by the organizer.

## 9.1 General Conduct
Maintain believable roleplay and driving. Trolling, unrealistic driving, desync abuse, outside-party interference, and combat logging are prohibited. Follow the announced participant, vehicle, equipment, and route limits.

## 9.2 Event Authorization
The supplied rules state that convoy events operate under an active **Section 144 authorization**, allowing PD aggressive engagement when required. Management should define what Section 144 means in the server, who activates it, how it is announced, and when it expires. Do not assume one authorization applies to unrelated events.

## 9.3 Police Authority and Interception
During an authorized convoy, PD may engage in a shootout at any location where the authorization and scenario permit it; approach from any direction; use PIT manoeuvres against convoy/family vehicles; disable convoy vehicles and proceed with tactical engagement; and use available air support and coordinated interception. These permissions do not allow unrelated or arbitrary targeting. General RP and PD procedures still apply.
```

Unrealistic driving, exploitative terrain use, deliberate desync abuse, and unreachable vehicle hiding are prohibited. PD's event-specific PIT permission does not create general permission for VDM or unrelated vehicle attacks.

Do not revive a player during an active gunfight. Wait until combat has ended and the applicable recovery rules permit revival. Do not use revival to return someone to an ongoing engagement. The organizer should clarify when combat is officially over if disputed.

**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 10 — GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEE PROTECTION**
**Discord message 1/2**

The supplied rules give additional protection to PD and EMS while performing government duties. These restrictions apply during ordinary roleplay, robberies, gang conflicts, and criminal scenarios.

## 10.1 PD and EMS Protection
Police officers and EMS personnel are not targets for arbitrary violence. Respect their role and safety while continuing to follow valid roleplay rules.

## 10.2 Hostage Restrictions
**PD and EMS personnel cannot be taken hostage under any circumstances.** Do not abduct, hold, threaten to exchange, or use them as hostages during a robbery, heist, gang conflict, or other scenario. When a hostage is required, use an eligible participant under the robbery rules.

## 10.3 Prohibited Targeting
Unnecessary targeting, random killing, and harassment of covered employees are prohibited. Do not target a person solely because they work for PD/EMS or because they participated in a separate scene. Hostile conduct must satisfy the applicable roleplay rules and have the required justification.

## 10.4 Valid RP Justification
Killing a protected employee without a valid, proper roleplay reason is prohibited. A disagreement, grudge, or desire to prevent an employee from doing their job does not automatically create a valid reason. Staff should review the whole scene and available evidence.

Report suspected violations through the designated process, preserve evidence, and let staff review the full context rather than escalating disputes during a live scene.

Use the server's ticket/reporting process. Explain what happened, when, the scenario involved, and the rule you believe applies. Attach POV, timestamps, or other evidence when available. Each player is responsible for their own conduct, even when acting with a gang, community, department, leader, or friend. A leader's instruction does not automatically excuse a violation. Gang GLP action applies only where the gang rules provide for it.

## 11.4 Administrative Actions
Depending on the rule and severity, staff may remove a player from an active scene, apply a published ban or job penalty, deduct GLP, suspend gang activities, revoke a base, or recommend dismantling where authorized. Actions should identify the rule and penalty. This handbook does not authorize an unlisted punishment.

Do not exploit vague wording, technical limits, or gaps to gain an advantage. Conversely, unclear wording should not silently become an extra penalty. Use the clearest existing rule and refer unresolved policy questions to management.

## 11.6 Self-Defence and Immediate Threats
Review self-defence claims in the actual context. They do not automatically override RDM, Fear RP, third-party involvement, civilian-versus-gang, robbery, or government employee protection rules. Preserve evidence and allow staff to assess competing accounts.

## 11.7 Administrative Decisions and Review
Staff may take immediate action to stop an ongoing violation or protect a live scene. Final administrative decisions rest with the Admin Team under the supplied rules. Players may submit a ticket for further review and should not interrupt a live scenario to contest the decision.
