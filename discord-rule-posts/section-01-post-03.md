**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 1 — GENERAL ROLEPLAY RULES**
*Post 3 of 5*

## 1.9 New Life Rule (NLR)
After death or when a new-life restriction applies, do not return to the same active situation to continue the conflict or use knowledge your character should no longer possess. Follow staff instructions about when you may return. The source provides no universal radius or timer. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.10 Fear RP / Value of Life
React reasonably to credible danger and value your character's life. Do not ignore serious threats merely to win or escape. Assess the actual threat and available options. Fear for police also applies where the circumstances require it. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.11 Combat Logging
Do not deliberately disconnect or leave the server during an active scene to avoid consequences. If a genuine technical failure occurs, return promptly and explain it to staff; the claim must be assessed against the circumstances. **Penalty: 7-day ban.**

## 1.12 Safe Zones
Do not begin prohibited violence in a designated safe zone or use the boundary to escape an active hostile scenario. Follow the zones and restrictions officially announced by the server; this handbook does not add locations. **Penalty: 3-day ban.**

## 1.13 Character Rules
Maintain continuity in character identity and behaviour. Do not switch characters or identities to evade an active scene, consequences, cooldowns, or a gang dismantling. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**
