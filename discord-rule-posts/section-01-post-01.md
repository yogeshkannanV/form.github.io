**ETERNITY SERVER | SECTION 1 — GENERAL ROLEPLAY RULES**
*Post 1 of 5*

These standards apply to all players and scenarios unless an approved event rule expressly changes a requirement. Keep roleplay believable, continuous, and fair; do not treat every interaction as a contest to win.

## 1.1 General Conduct and Respect
Respect players and staff. Do not derail a live scenario to argue about a ruling; preserve evidence and use the ticket process afterwards. Do not abuse game mechanics, technical problems, or rule loopholes to gain an unfair advantage. **Penalty: 3-day ban; severe cases may be permanent.** Chat-specific rules are not included in this handbook.

## 1.2 Roleplay Standards
Act consistently with your character and the scene. Respond to meaningful events, allow other players a chance to react, and avoid actions that skip required interaction or force a preferred outcome. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.3 Fail Roleplay (FRP)
FRP is conduct that breaks the reasonable logic or continuity of a scene, ignores relevant circumstances, or uses game limitations instead of believable roleplay. Speaking to or interacting with a dead body is treated as FRP in the supplied rules, but the gang rules give a different penalty for dead-body interaction; see Sections 7 and 11. **General penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.4 Metagaming (MG)
Do not use information your character did not obtain through roleplay. Information from external chats, streams, recordings, or out-of-character conversations must not influence character decisions until it is established in character. **Penalty: 5-day ban.**
