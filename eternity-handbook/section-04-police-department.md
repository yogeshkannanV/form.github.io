# Section 4 — Police Department Rules

Police enforce the city's rules while maintaining fair, believable roleplay. Rank, equipment, or police access does not exempt an officer from server-wide rules.

## 4.1 Professional Conduct
Follow department procedures and authorized command instructions. Treat civilians, suspects, hostages, EMS, and other departments professionally. Do not use police status, access, or information for personal disputes or private gain. Every action must relate to a valid RP reason and current scenario.

## 4.2 Appropriate Use of Force
Use only the force supported by the circumstances and applicable SOP. Do not escalate to lethal force without a valid reason; consider whether negotiation, communication, containment, or another reasonable response is available. The source does not include a complete force ladder, so follow current PD SOP and approved event instructions.

## 4.3 Fear for Police
Officers are still roleplay participants and must react believably to credible danger. Do not ignore serious threats simply because you have a badge or equipment. Fear RP applies in accordance with the situation and general rules.

## 4.4 Protection of Police Bodies
The supplied rules specifically prohibit burning police bodies. Do not burn or handle a police body in a prohibited way. If the rule is unclear in a particular scene, avoid exploiting it and follow staff instructions.

## 4.5 Government Employee Protection
PD and EMS are protected under Section 10 and cannot be taken hostage under any circumstances. Unnecessary targeting, random killing, harassment, or killing without valid roleplay justification may result in a **15-day ban without exception**.

## 4.6 Police Backup
During robberies, backup may be called if all involved officers are down or dead after a realistic delay; no universal timer is specified. During authorized convoys, Section 9's event permissions apply. Do not use backup to bypass participant limits or manipulate a scenario.

## 4.7 Department Violations
Confirmed abuse of authority, failure to follow procedures, or unnecessary force may result in **removal from PD and a 3-day ban**. Staff should review evidence, identify the rule applied, and document the decision. Requests for further review use the ticket process.
