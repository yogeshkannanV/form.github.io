# Section 12 — Final Notice

## 12.1 Player Responsibilities
Every player is responsible for reading and following this handbook, current server rules, and approved event instructions. If a rule is unclear, ask staff before relying on an interpretation that could affect a scenario. Not knowing a rule is not a reliable reason to disregard it.

## 12.2 Leadership Accountability
Gang leaders, department command staff, and event organizers must ensure members understand requirements before participating. Leaders must not instruct others to bypass restrictions, interfere in unauthorized situations, or ignore evidence requests. Individual responsibility still applies to every member.

## 12.3 Handbook Compliance
This handbook covers general roleplay, POV, robbery, PD, EMS, community, gang, GCA, convoy, government protection, and administrative rules. Additional event conditions must be communicated before the event and must not silently override unrelated server-wide rules.

Where two written rules conflict, do not invent a penalty or assume the harshest outcome applies automatically. Refer the issue to management and publish a written decision.

## 12.4 Enforcement Policy
Staff may take immediate steps to stop an ongoing violation, review evidence, and apply the consequence stated in the applicable published rule. Final administrative decisions rest with the Admin Team; players may use the ticket process for further review. Penalties must follow published policy.

## 12.5 Management Approval Checklist
Before making this handbook final, management should confirm:
- Which penalty takes priority when general and gang rules overlap.
- The official GCA boundary, timer-expiry, and victory conditions.
- Whether street-fight participant limits are per team or combined.
- The gang participant cap for approved civilian-versus-gang scenarios.
- Whether third-party suspension and the 100-GLP deduction are cumulative.
- The approved weapon list and event-specific GCA/convoy limits.
- The intended meaning of the asset/FRP rule when fewer than two gang members are online.
