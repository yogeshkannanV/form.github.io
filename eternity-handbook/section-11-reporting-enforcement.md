# Section 11 — Reporting and Administrative Enforcement

Report suspected violations through the designated process, preserve evidence, and let staff review the full context rather than escalating disputes during a live scene.

## 11.1 Reporting
Use the server's ticket/reporting process. Explain what happened, when, the scenario involved, and the rule you believe applies. Attach POV, timestamps, or other evidence when available. Do not submit fabricated or misleading evidence. An allegation is not proof. The source gives no universal ticket template or response deadline; follow the current instructions.

## 11.2 Evidence Review
Follow Section 2. Staff should distinguish what evidence clearly establishes from what remains uncertain, and request further material where appropriate. Players must comply with valid POV requests; low-spec hardware alone is not an automatic exemption.

## 11.3 Individual Responsibility
Each player is responsible for their own conduct, even when acting with a gang, community, department, leader, or friend. A leader's instruction does not automatically excuse a violation. Gang GLP action applies only where the gang rules provide for it.

## 11.4 Administrative Actions
Depending on the rule and severity, staff may remove a player from an active scene, apply a published ban or job penalty, deduct GLP, suspend gang activities, revoke a base, or recommend dismantling where authorized. Actions should identify the rule and penalty. This handbook does not authorize an unlisted punishment.

## 11.5 No Loophole Abuse
Do not exploit vague wording, technical limits, or gaps to gain an advantage. Conversely, unclear wording should not silently become an extra penalty. Use the clearest existing rule and refer unresolved policy questions to management.

## 11.6 Self-Defence and Immediate Threats
Review self-defence claims in the actual context. They do not automatically override RDM, Fear RP, third-party involvement, civilian-versus-gang, robbery, or government employee protection rules. Preserve evidence and allow staff to assess competing accounts.

## 11.7 Administrative Decisions and Review
Staff may take immediate action to stop an ongoing violation or protect a live scene. Final administrative decisions rest with the Admin Team under the supplied rules. Players may submit a ticket for further review and should not interrupt a live scenario to contest the decision.

## 11.8 Known Conflicts Requiring Management Decision
- General RDM: 7-day ban; gang RDM: 4-day ban.
- General FRP: 24-hour ban; gang dead-body interaction: 7-day ban and 50 GLP deduction.
- Robbery-location shootout: 24-hour ban in one entry and 3-day ban in another.
- Third-party gang interference: 3-day suspension and 100-GLP deduction in separate entries.
- GCA zone boundary and victory conditions conflict in wording.
- Civilian limit is 4, but the maximum number of gang participants in approved civilian-versus-gang scenarios is missing.

Do not automatically combine conflicting penalties; management should publish one authoritative interpretation.

## 11.9 Rule Updates
When management changes a rule, update the relevant handbook page and announce the change. Where practical, record the effective date and whether the change affects a restriction, a penalty, or both.
