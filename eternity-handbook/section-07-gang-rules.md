# Section 7 — Gang Rules

These rules apply to registered gangs and members acting on their behalf. Leadership must communicate limits and prevent members from bypassing the approved rules.

## 7.1 Registration and Leadership
Every gang requires approval from the Hood Organizer. Leaders must provide accurate registration information and follow roster requirements. They should brief members on event caps, cooldowns, base restrictions, and evidence requirements. Each member remains responsible for their own conduct.

## 7.2 Gang Operational Bases
Bases are allocated by the Hood Organizer based on availability; the allocation decision is final unless reopened by management. Do not begin or end a scenario at your base; takeovers cannot be conducted there; robberies must not be moved there or deliberately ended there. Keep base activity discreet and do not expose it to police to manipulate a scenario. Do not access a vault during an active scenario to replenish equipment. Confirmed violations may lead to GLP reduction or loss of base access.

Base payment is due every **15 days**. If payment is missed and remains unpaid after a final reminder, management may reclaim the base.

## 7.3 Gang Life Points (GLP)
Maximum: **250 GLP** per gang. Confirmed FRP or other approved gang-level violations may cause a deduction after evidence review by the Gang Organizer. Allegations alone are not proof. Document the rule and deduction.

| GLP level | Stated consequence |
|---|---|
| Below 200 | Applicable tier perks removed |
| Below 150 | Special-vehicle restrictions and 2-week gang RP suspension |
| Below 25 | Gang must dismantle, subject to management confirmation |

GLP may be earned through approved underworld events such as illegal car expos, boxing, and illegal races. Points depend on participation and criteria announced by the organizer.

## 7.4 Dismantling and Cooldowns
Members of a dismantled gang may join another gang after a **14-day cooldown** and must not use the old identity to bypass dismantling. New registration may be submitted after **30 days** with a distinct identity, subject to approval.

After main war/breach with the same gang, the stated cooldown is **3 days**. After a war/breach, wait **6 hours** before engaging a different gang. Management should clarify exactly when a cooldown begins and how cancellations count.

## 7.5 Third-Party Involvement
Do not join or support an active gang scenario unless your gang is officially involved or management approves it. No unapproved reinforcements, lookouts, weapons, vehicles, or tactical assistance. If Gang X and Gang Y are in conflict, Gang Z must not support either side without permission. Source penalties are a **3-day gang suspension** and, in another entry, a **100 GLP deduction**. Management must confirm whether these are cumulative or alternative.

## 7.6 Gang RDM and Dead Bodies
Gang RDM is prohibited; the gang-specific penalty is **4 days**, but general RDM is **7 days**. Management must clarify which applies when both rules fit.

For speaking to/interacting with a dead body, the gang rule specifies **50 GLP deducted and a 7-day player ban**. The general FRP rule separately lists dead-body interaction with a 24-hour penalty. Management must resolve the conflict.

## 7.7 Gang Vehicles and Assets
Using another gang's vehicles or assets without permission carries a stated **3-day ban**. The source says a gang's own assets may be used inside the city when **two or more gang members are online**. It also says FRP in these asset situations should not be considered if fewer than two members are online; that wording is ambiguous and is not a general exemption from FRP.

## 7.8 Street Fight Limits
The source gives a minimum of **15** and maximum of **25** participants. When the event has 25 members, the stated vehicle limit is **8 vehicles total**, including no more than **4 premium-pack PIT vehicles**. It is unclear whether participants are counted per side or overall; organizers must announce the interpretation.

## 7.9 Gang Fight Scheduling

- **Monday to Friday:** gang-versus-gang fights are strictly prohibited during city events unless management grants permission in advance. A gang must not initiate or engage in a prohibited fight without that approval.
- **Saturday and Sunday:** organized gang fights may take place only after registration and approval through the server's official process.
- Follow the date, time, location, roster, and event conditions confirmed by management. Do not treat weekend availability as automatic approval.

## 7.10 Main War and Location
Main war format: **12 vs. 12**, with **4 premium PIT vehicles and 1 common vehicle**. The war location should be chosen jointly at the official meeting; the source lists **Sandy Shores or Paleto Bay**. Do not change the approved location without permission.

## 7.11 Takeovers and GCA
GCA locations can be conquered only through an approved takeover initiated by two teams. A gang may attempt a second GCA location only if it already controls one. Abandoned locations return to the Hood Organizer. Section 8 sets out zone and victory wording that needs management clarification.

## 7.12 Civilian-versus-Gang
Such scenarios require management approval. The civilian cap is **4**; the source does not clearly specify the gang cap. Unauthorized scenarios carry a proposed **15-day ban for all involved**.

## 7.13 Penalty Summary
Gang RDM: 4-day ban (conflicts with general RDM); unauthorized use of another gang's assets: 3-day ban; third-party interference: 3-day suspension and/or 100 GLP; dead-body interaction: 50 GLP and 7-day player ban in the gang entry; under 200 GLP removes perks; under 150 GLP restricts special vehicles and suspends gang RP for two weeks; under 25 GLP requires dismantling. Management should resolve overlapping penalties before final publication.
