# Section 3 — Robbery and Heist Rules

Robberies and heists must develop through roleplay, not become immediate unstructured shootouts. Criminals, hostages, and police must respect the same active scenario.

## 3.1 General Requirements
Follow the police-presence and hostage requirements for the robbery category. The supplied rules require PD and a hostage but do not give one universal count. Allow negotiation where required; do not shoot instantly just to skip interaction. Do not involve unrelated players. General robbery violations carry a stated **3-day ban**, subject to the more specific rules below.

## 3.2 Participation From the Start
A player or group must be involved from the beginning of the robbery/heist to participate. Do not arrive late and join because the event looks advantageous. Outside parties may not interfere unless event rules or staff explicitly authorize them.

## 3.3 Police Backup
Backup may be called if all officers involved are down or dead, after a realistic delay for the scene to develop and robbers to collect or proceed. The source gives no fixed universal timer. Do not exploit the delay by camping indefinitely; police may engage through backup where event conditions permit.

## 3.4 Vehicle Restrictions
Do not swap vehicles through a garage between related scenarios to reset pursuit or escape restrictions. Use permitted vehicles and respect announced limits. Do not use unreachable locations, vehicle exploits, or unrealistic manoeuvres to make pursuit impossible.

## 3.5 Suppressed Fire and Communication
The supplied rules say criminals are not always required to use verbal communication, but suppressed fire is required before firing at PD once pursuit starts. Suppressed fire is also stated to be mandatory if a shootout occurs at the robbery location. Staff should clarify any weapon exceptions before an event. A verbal warning in a civilian-to-civilian confrontation may be considered, but does not itself justify violence.

## 3.6 Cooldowns and Multiple Robberies
The same team must not conduct multiple robberies simultaneously. Observe the relevant category cooldown. The source does not give one universal duration; use the current published rule or event announcement. Do not use an interrupted scenario to bypass a cooldown unless staff confirms it does not apply.

## 3.7 Holding a Robbery Location
The source uses a first-come, first-served approach. If the first team is holding the location while waiting for a cooldown or next permitted stage, a second team must leave. Do not manufacture a shootout over location ownership.

## 3.8 No Shootouts at Robbery Locations
One source entry specifies a **24-hour ban** if teams engage in a prohibited shootout at the robbery location. A later rule prohibits shootouts at the same location and lists a **3-day ban**. These penalties conflict; management must clarify which applies and to whom.

## 3.9 Hostage Protection
Hostages are participants in the scene, not disposable props. Do not kill a hostage to avoid negotiation or escape consequences. **Penalty for killing a hostage: 5-day ban.**

## 3.10 Penalty Summary

| Violation | Stated consequence |
|---|---|
| General robbery violation | 3-day ban |
| Prohibited shootout at robbery location | 24-hour or 3-day ban in separate rules; management clarification required |
| Killing a hostage | 5-day ban |
| Other vehicle, cooldown, or participation violation | Apply the published rule; no universal duration provided |

Before starting, know the robbery category, required PD/hostage conditions, vehicle and suppressor rules, cooldown, and staff contact process.
