# Section 1 — General Roleplay Rules

These standards apply to all players and scenarios unless an approved event rule expressly changes a requirement. Keep roleplay believable, continuous, and fair; do not treat every interaction as a contest to win.

## 1.1 General Conduct and Respect
Respect players and staff. Do not derail a live scenario to argue about a ruling; preserve evidence and use the ticket process afterwards. Do not abuse game mechanics, technical problems, or rule loopholes to gain an unfair advantage. **Penalty: 3-day ban; severe cases may be permanent.** Chat-specific rules are not included in this handbook.

## 1.2 Roleplay Standards
Act consistently with your character and the scene. Respond to meaningful events, allow other players a chance to react, and avoid actions that skip required interaction or force a preferred outcome. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.3 Fail Roleplay (FRP)
FRP is conduct that breaks the reasonable logic or continuity of a scene, ignores relevant circumstances, or uses game limitations instead of believable roleplay. Speaking to or interacting with a dead body is treated as FRP in the supplied rules, but the gang rules give a different penalty for dead-body interaction; see Sections 7 and 11. **General penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.4 Metagaming (MG)
Do not use information your character did not obtain through roleplay. Information from external chats, streams, recordings, or out-of-character conversations must not influence character decisions until it is established in character. **Penalty: 5-day ban.**

## 1.5 Powergaming (PG)
Do not force an outcome on another player without a reasonable opportunity to respond, claim unrealistic abilities, or use mechanics to obtain an implausible result. Describe the attempted action and allow the roleplay to develop. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.6 Random Deathmatch (RDM)
Do not attack or kill a player without the required roleplay context or valid scenario justification. A rival identity or previous dispute does not automatically justify every later attack. **General penalty: 7-day ban.** Gang RDM is listed as 4 days separately; management must clarify priority when both apply.

## 1.7 Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)
Do not deliberately use a vehicle to hit, run over, or kill players without valid roleplay justification. A permitted PIT manoeuvre in an authorized convoy is a specific event permission, not a general exemption. **Penalty: 3-day ban.**

## 1.8 Car Deathmatch (CDM)
Prohibited vehicle attacks intended to harm players or disrupt a scene may be treated as CDM. Staff should consider intent, context, and whether an event expressly permits the manoeuvre. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.** The supplied rule says there is no appeal for clear evidence; evidence must still be reviewed through the administrative process.

## 1.9 New Life Rule (NLR)
After death or when a new-life restriction applies, do not return to the same active situation to continue the conflict or use knowledge your character should no longer possess. Follow staff instructions about when you may return. The source provides no universal radius or timer. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.10 Fear RP / Value of Life
React reasonably to credible danger and value your character's life. Do not ignore serious threats merely to win or escape. Assess the actual threat and available options. Fear for police also applies where the circumstances require it. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.11 Combat Logging
Do not deliberately disconnect or leave the server during an active scene to avoid consequences. If a genuine technical failure occurs, return promptly and explain it to staff; the claim must be assessed against the circumstances. **Penalty: 7-day ban.**

## 1.12 Safe Zones
Do not begin prohibited violence in a designated safe zone or use the boundary to escape an active hostile scenario. Follow the zones and restrictions officially announced by the server; this handbook does not add locations. **Penalty: 3-day ban.**

## 1.13 Character Rules
Maintain continuity in character identity and behaviour. Do not switch characters or identities to evade an active scene, consequences, cooldowns, or a gang dismantling. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**

## 1.14 Voice and Immersion Rules
Use voice communication in a way that supports the scene and allows participants to understand relevant interaction. Explain genuine technical issues to staff rather than using them for advantage. **Penalty: 12-hour ban.**

## 1.15 Exploits and Cheating
Cheats, unauthorized modifications, exploit abuse, and intentional bug manipulation for advantage are prohibited. Stop using an exploit and report it. **Penalty: permanent ban.**

## 1.16 Unrealistic Roleplay (URP)
URP includes plainly unrealistic actions that undermine the setting or exploit game limitations instead of responding through roleplay. **Penalty: 2-hour ban; severe cases may be permanent.**

## 1.17 Cop Baiting
Do not deliberately provoke police to manufacture an artificial pursuit without a reasonable roleplay basis. **Penalty: 3-day ban, no warning.**

## 1.18 Lore Breaking
Keep conduct consistent with the server's setting and accepted roleplay lore. **Penalty: 3-day ban; repeated violations may be permanent.**

## 1.19 Ruleplay
Do not exploit technical wording or loopholes to defeat a rule's purpose. If a rule is genuinely unclear, ask staff instead of adopting an aggressive interpretation during a live scene. **Penalty: 24-hour ban.**
