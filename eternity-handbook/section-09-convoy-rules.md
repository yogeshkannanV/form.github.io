# Section 9 — Convoy Rules

A convoy is a structured roleplay event involving an assigned route, a consignment, authorized participants, police interception, and conditions announced by the organizer.

## 9.1 General Conduct
Maintain believable roleplay and driving. Trolling, unrealistic driving, desync abuse, outside-party interference, and combat logging are prohibited. Follow the announced participant, vehicle, equipment, and route limits.

## 9.2 Event Authorization
The supplied rules state that convoy events operate under an active **Section 144 authorization**, allowing PD aggressive engagement when required. Management should define what Section 144 means in the server, who activates it, how it is announced, and when it expires. Do not assume one authorization applies to unrelated events.

## 9.3 Police Authority and Interception
During an authorized convoy, PD may engage in a shootout at any location where the authorization and scenario permit it; approach from any direction; use PIT manoeuvres against convoy/family vehicles; disable convoy vehicles and proceed with tactical engagement; and use available air support and coordinated interception. These permissions do not allow unrelated or arbitrary targeting. General RP and PD procedures still apply.

## 9.4 Gang and Family Responsibilities
Follow the route assigned by Black Squad or the designated organizer. Do not change it without authorization or deliberately evade it. Families may defend the consignment with permitted manpower and strategy but must remain within announced limits. Do not hide vehicles in unreachable/exploitative locations or bring unapproved gangs/families as allies.

The source gives no universal convoy participant cap, vehicle count, or weapon list. The organizer must announce these for every event; do not assume GCA caps apply automatically.

## 9.5 Route Requirements
The organizer should communicate the assigned route before departure. Participants must not detour, hide, or exploit map geometry to avoid the event. If an unexpected blockage occurs, ask staff for direction rather than independently redefining the route.

## 9.6 Vehicles and Driving
Unrealistic driving, exploitative terrain use, deliberate desync abuse, and unreachable vehicle hiding are prohibited. PD's event-specific PIT permission does not create general permission for VDM or unrelated vehicle attacks.

## 9.7 Reviving
Do not revive a player during an active gunfight. Wait until combat has ended and the applicable recovery rules permit revival. Do not use revival to return someone to an ongoing engagement. The organizer should clarify when combat is officially over if disputed.

## 9.8 Safe Zones and Third Parties
Do not use a safe zone to escape a convoy engagement. No third-party alliance is permitted unless approved before the scenario. Assistance includes fighting, blocking routes, scouting, tactical communications, or supplying equipment to change the outcome.

## 9.9 Combat Logging
Do not disconnect to avoid interception, defeat, arrest, or the consequences of the active scenario. If a genuine technical failure occurs, return promptly and explain it to staff; the circumstances and evidence should be reviewed.

## 9.10 Disqualification and Penalties
Confirmed violations may lead to immediate disqualification and the applicable server punishment. The supplied rules do not assign one fixed ban length for every convoy violation. Apply the general penalty table or an event-specific penalty announced in advance; do not invent a duration during the event.

## 9.11 Organizer Checklist
Before departure, announce Section 144 authorization, organizer, route, participants, vehicle/equipment caps, PD authority, revival restrictions, third-party policy, and when the event officially ends.
