# Section 10 — Government Employee Protection

The supplied rules give additional protection to PD and EMS while performing government duties. These restrictions apply during ordinary roleplay, robberies, gang conflicts, and criminal scenarios.

## 10.1 PD and EMS Protection
Police officers and EMS personnel are not targets for arbitrary violence. Respect their role and safety while continuing to follow valid roleplay rules.

## 10.2 Hostage Restrictions
**PD and EMS personnel cannot be taken hostage under any circumstances.** Do not abduct, hold, threaten to exchange, or use them as hostages during a robbery, heist, gang conflict, or other scenario. When a hostage is required, use an eligible participant under the robbery rules.

## 10.3 Prohibited Targeting
Unnecessary targeting, random killing, and harassment of covered employees are prohibited. Do not target a person solely because they work for PD/EMS or because they participated in a separate scene. Hostile conduct must satisfy the applicable roleplay rules and have the required justification.

## 10.4 Valid RP Justification
Killing a protected employee without a valid, proper roleplay reason is prohibited. A disagreement, grudge, or desire to prevent an employee from doing their job does not automatically create a valid reason. Staff should review the whole scene and available evidence.

## 10.5 Punishment
The supplied rule specifies a **15-day ban without exception** for violating government employee protection. Staff should document the applicable rule and evidence. If another rule overlaps, apply the published policy rather than adding unlisted penalties.
