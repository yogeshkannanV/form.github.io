# Section 6 — Community Rules

Communities and civilian groups must remain peaceful unless management has approved a specific roleplay scenario. Do not use a community as a cover for gang warfare, criminal operations, or coordinated interference.

## 6.1 Peaceful Conduct
Community activity should support ordinary social roleplay. Keep it separate from registered gang warfare and hostile scenarios unless an event is approved.

## 6.2 No Community Gang Warfare
Do not conduct gang warfare under a community name to bypass registration, participant caps, or cooldowns. Changing the label of an active gang conflict does not change the rules that apply.

## 6.3 No Provocation
Do not deliberately provoke or threaten another community to manufacture a hostile situation. A past disagreement does not justify targeting people who are not involved in the current scene.

## 6.4 No Interference in Gang Conflicts
Do not provide reinforcements, lookouts, weapons, vehicles, tactical information, or other help intended to influence an ongoing gang scenario. Do not join because a friend or community member is involved unless you are an authorized participant.

## 6.5 No Forced Recruitment
Do not force or coerce players into joining a community, group, or gang. Recruitment must be voluntary and consistent with RP standards.

## 6.6 No Collective Retaliation
Do not retaliate against an entire community for one person's actions. Keep disputes connected to the participants actually involved and report suspected rule violations through the approved process.

## 6.7 Civilian-versus-Gang Restrictions
Civilian-versus-gang scenarios are prohibited without management approval. The source caps civilians at **4**, but the gang participant maximum is unclear and must be published by management. Unauthorized scenarios carry a proposed **15-day ban for all involved**.

## 6.8 No Third-Party Assistance
Do not form unapproved alliances to influence a conflict. Assistance includes fighting, blocking routes, scouting, supplying equipment, or coordinating actions that materially affect the result. If you are not part of the approved scenario, stay out.

## 6.9 Self-Defence
Fear RP and the rules covering RDM, third-party involvement, and civilian-versus-gang scenarios still apply. A self-defence claim must be reviewed in context and is not a blanket exemption from community rules.
